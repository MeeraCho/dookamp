# DooKamp

**Understand what schools teach. Learn in English and your home language.**

DooKamp is a curriculum-based learning platform that makes foundational knowledge taught in Canadian and American elementary schools accessible to learners of different ages and language backgrounds.

- **For Students:** Explore school subjects, understand core concepts, and build academic vocabulary through clear explanations and engaging learning activities.
- **For Parents:** Understand what your children are learning at school. Even if English is not your first language, home-language explanations help you understand their lessons and support their learning at home.
- **For English Learners:** Build English vocabulary and understand foundational concepts commonly taught in North American elementary schools, using familiar concepts and home-language support when needed.
